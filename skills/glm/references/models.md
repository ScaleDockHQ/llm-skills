## Context

- GLM-5.2, 5.3 and 5.3-Flash: 1,048,576 tokens native, no YaRN needed. Maximum output on the hosted GLM-5.3 is 128,000 tokens (The New Stack, press); the cards use 128K max new tokens in agent evals.
- Whether 1M fits is a KV-cache question: the vLLM recipe fits it on 8x B200 with FP8 KV cache and `--max-num-seqs 32`; GLM-5.3-Flash's hybrid attention keeps the KV small.
- GLM-4.7 and 4.7-Flash: 202,752 positions in `config.json`.
