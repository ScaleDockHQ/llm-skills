## General gotchas

- Do not keep old thoughts in the message history (model card rule). Clients that echo `reasoning_content` back into `messages` degrade multi-turn quality.
- E2B/E4B behave differently from larger sizes when thinking is off (no empty thought block).
- Ollama short tags (`gemma4`, `gemma4:26b`) have been repointed; pin the full tag or digest.
- Gemma 4 on DGX Spark depends on build: forum reports vary from 10-12 tok/s (eager mode, stock container) to ~40 tok/s (community Docker image) for the 26B A4B.
