# Qwen known issues

> Verified against QwenLM/Qwen3.8 issues #216, #236, #238, #244 and the open-issue list, llama.cpp issue #27107 and discussion #27281, unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF discussion 71, vLLM recipes (Qwen3.8-27B, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next), SGLang Qwen3.8 cookbook, TensorRT-LLM Qwen3.8 guide, lmstudio-bug-tracker #2345, 2026-10-11

Issue status moves fast. Re-open the linked page before telling a user a bug is still present or fixed.

## Gaps and unverified

- No official Qwen GGUF or MLX for 3.8 or 3.6; all GGUF and MLX come from third parties. Official GGUF exists for Qwen3-Coder-Next, Qwen3-Next-80B, Qwen3-VL and Qwen3-235B-A22B.
- The SGLang cookbook page for Qwen3.8-27B and for Flash-Next was not read (only the 2.4T cookbook and the card commands). Flash-Next SGLang flags beyond the card command are unverified.
- TokenSpeed commands were copied from the cards; not run or read in its own docs.
- TensorFold support, Ollama coverage beyond `qwen3.8:27b`, and Ollama tags for Flash-Next and the 2.4T were not confirmed.
- TensorRT-LLM support matrix has a `Qwen4ExpForConditionalGeneration` row (presumably Flash-Next); its status columns were not decoded.
- Legacy Qwen3 (2025) tool-call parser name for vLLM and SGLang was not confirmed from an engine doc. Qwen3-Coder-Next cards say `qwen3_coder`.
- Qwen3.5 122B-A10B and 35B-A3B licenses, Qwen3-2507 cards, WebWorld, Qwen-AgentWorld and Qwen-Drive cards were not read.
- DGX Spark throughput for 27B NVFP4 was not found in the sources; the recipe only lists GB10 as a supported target.
