# Qwen platforms and quants

> Verified against vLLM recipes (Qwen3.8-27B, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Qwen index), SGLang Qwen3.8 cookbook, TensorRT-LLM Qwen3.8/3.5 guide, NVIDIA Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4 card, Unsloth Qwen3.8 guide, Ollama qwen3.8 page, ggml-org and unsloth GGUF cards, Hugging Face Qwen3.8 repo search, TensorFold skill (v1.0.7 notes), 2026-10-11

Sizes are weight sizes from the cited pages; add KV cache, recurrent state and (for vision) encoder memory. Qwen3.8 attention is mostly linear, so KV is small, but SGLang and TensorRT-LLM size a separate recurrent-state pool that often caps concurrency.

## NVIDIA DGX Spark (GB10, 128 GB unified)

| Model                      | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Qwen3.8-27B                | vLLM with `nvidia/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` (32 GB) or `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8` (38 GB). BF16 (67 GB) also fits                                                              | The vLLM recipe lists DGX Spark (GB10, 128G) as a hardware target. Unsloth reports NVFP4 about 1.5x faster than BF16 on B200; Spark numbers not read, so measure |
| Qwen3.8-27B via TensorFold | Community MiaAI-Lab recipe, MLX 4-bit plus DFlash2 drafter, one Spark                                                                                               | See the tensorfold skill; recipe pins TensorFold v0.6.0 with patches                                                                                             |
| Qwen3.8-Flash-Next         | Does not fit one Spark in NVFP4 or FP8 (about 124 to 130 GB NVFP4). Two Sparks: community TensorFold TP=2 recipe (NVFP4 or INT4-AutoRound), 1,048,576-token context | See the tensorfold skill `combos/dgx-spark-dual.md`. A single-Spark GGUF Q4 (about 104 GB) was not tested here                                                   |
| Qwen3.8-2.4T               | Not Spark territory                                                                                                                                                 | 16 B200/B300/GB200/GB300 minimum for FP8 on TensorRT-LLM                                                                                                         |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B            | vLLM FP8 (42 GB) or NVFP4 (21 GB)                                                                                                                                   | Recipe sizes                                                                                                                                                     |

Run Qwen engines on Spark in an exclusive llama-swap group; never co-locate other GPU engines (`self-hosted-llms`).

## Consumer RTX

| GPU                          | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 16 to 24 GB (RTX 5080, 4090) | Qwen3.8-27B 4-bit GGUF (Unsloth: 16 to 19 GB, plus 1 to 2 GB if MTP is used) in llama.cpp. Blackwell cards (RTX 50): Unsloth NVFP4 in vLLM, reported 1.5x BF16 speed on 24 GB                                                |
| 32 GB, one RTX 5090          | vLLM `Inferact/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` with `--enforce-eager --kv-cache-dtype fp8 --max-model-len 32768`. Without `--enforce-eager` CUDA graph capture runs out of memory (31.4 GiB usable). Verified by the recipe               |
| 2x RTX 5090                  | vLLM TP2, `unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` or Inferact NVFP4 or FP8, 262,144 context, `--kv-cache-dtype fp8`. Unsloth build leaves about twice the KV cache (920,517 vs 445,875 tokens measured); Inferact drafts better with MTP |
| RTX Pro 6000 (96 GB)         | Listed in the 27B recipe hardware list; FP8 or BF16 single card is plausible, not measured on the page                                                                                                                       |

Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on consumer cards: not a fit (tens to 100+ GB); only CPU-offloaded GGUF, untested here.

## Datacenter

| Hardware             | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1x H100 80 GB        | Qwen3.8-27B FP8 (38 GB; the recipe also lists BF16 67 GB and INT4 24 GB); vLLM TP1                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 4x H100 80 GB        | Flash-Next FP8, TP4 with `VLLM_PLE_CPU_OFFLOAD=1 --moe-backend triton`. Without offload plain TP4 OOMs at startup. Recipe measured about 1,430 output tok/s at concurrency 64 (1024 in, 256 out)                                                                                                                       |
| 8x H200              | Flash-Next FP8 with TEP8: `--tensor-parallel-size 8 --enable-expert-parallel --moe-backend triton`. Plain TP8 is incompatible with the FP8 block size                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 4x GB300             | Flash-Next FP8 TP4 (recommended), TP2 minimum; add `--kv-cache-dtype fp8 --attention-config.indexer_kv_dtype fp8 --no-enable-flashinfer-autotune --speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":3}'`                                                                                                   |
| B200 x4              | Flash-Next NVFP4 (`Inferact/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4`) TP4 with `--max-num-seqs 256`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2.4T-A95B FP8        | Multi-node only on NVIDIA at FP8: B200, B300, GB200 or GB300, 16 GPUs minimum in the TensorRT-LLM guide (TP16). SGLang: FP8 fits no single node (B300 8x288 GB is 2.30 TB), single-node needs FP4: NVFP4 on B300 or MXFP4 on MI355X. AMD: MI355X MXFP4 TP8 is the recommended single-node path; MI300X needs two nodes |
| AMD MI300X to MI355X | 27B and Flash-Next listed in vLLM recipes (Flash-Next FP8 TP4 on MI355X with `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1 VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER_MOE=0`)                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Huawei Ascend 950PR  | 27B via vLLM Ascend (W8A8 or native block FP8); outside this repo's focus                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Flash-Next vLLM prerequisites: host RAM of at least 51 GB plus headroom for n-gram offload; `--max-num-seqs 256` (the Mamba-cache capacity error appears otherwise); no pipeline parallelism; XPU and TPU unsupported.

## Speculative decoding

| Method                    | Engine       | Command or note                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Built-in MTP              | vLLM         | 3.8: `--speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":3}'`. Works in FP8, NVFP4 and the 5090 runs (acceptance 0.75 to 0.90 measured). 3.5 and 3.6 cards: `qwen3_next_mtp`, 2 tokens                             |
| Built-in MTP              | SGLang       | `--speculative-algorithm NEXTN` (or `EAGLE` per Unsloth for NVFP4): steps 3, topk 1, draft tokens 4. NEXTN caps concurrency at 48 unless `--max-running-requests` is set; not usable with pipeline parallelism (2.4T cookbook) |
| Built-in MTP              | TensorRT-LLM | `speculative_config.max_draft_len` 3 in the MTP3 curated profiles                                                                                                                                                              |
| DFlash2 or DSpark drafter | vLLM         | `--speculative-config '{"method":"dflash","model":"incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2","num_speculative_tokens":7}'` (vLLM 0.28.0+), `{"method":"dspark","model":"RedHatAI/Qwen3.8-27B-speculator.dspark","num_speculative_tokens":8}` |
| DSpark                    | SGLang 2.4T  | `--speculative-algorithm DSPARK --speculative-draft-model-path RadixArk/Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B-DSpark`, needs `--pp-size 1`                                                                                                         |

Benchmark MTP with a realistic dataset (vLLM recipe says SPEED-Bench, not `random`) and read acceptance from `vllm:spec_decode_num_accepted_tokens_total` and `..._draft_tokens_total`.
